# Real estate agency

A Kybernos theme for an estate agency. Data only: JSON and Markdown, no code, no media.

| What | Words and content |
|---|---|
| Business rows | Contacts, Properties, Sales and lettings |
| Contacts | buyers, sellers, landlords, tenants and partners, owner companies and partner firms |
| Catalog | one kind of item, the property, with for sale, to let, under offer, sold and mandate draft |
| Pipeline | two boards: Sales (enquiry to closed) and Lettings (application to moved in) |
| Kit | 2 skills, 1 team, 3 automations (created switched off), 4 goals taken from the `immo` pack |
| Style | the warm light "immobilier-locatif" preset |

`demo/sample.json` holds the sample rows of an invented agency ("Harbor & Pine Realty"): 8 contacts and 4 companies, 8 properties, 11 cards, 5 notes. Every name, address and message is made up; e-mail addresses are on `example.com`.

See `docs/dev/shipped-themes.md` for the folder shape and how to add another theme.
