Three 3D examples, each a small app

Every example is one self-contained page: a live scene you can turn, a legend whose numbers follow what you do, command buttons, and an Export that gives a real .glb. The grinder and the engine are CAD solids, checked before they ship; their mass, cost and pressure are computed, not drawn.

Drag to turn · pinch or ctrl-scroll to zoom

In the product, Export saves the .glb into the project and Use this request fills the composer with the example's request. Downloads are blocked inside this preview, so Export shows the real file size instead.
