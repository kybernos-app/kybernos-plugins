## Passed with a real measure (not a finding)

- J08-06 `scripts/test-stripe-replica-money.mjs` is built and 10 of 10 pass on the replica: a team plan paid; a top-up credited once; the same event redelivered changes nothing; a partial refund leaves the credit; a full refund and a dispute take back what was not spent and the audit shows `topup.revoke`; a refund delivered twice revokes once; a failed renewal puts the plan past due and a paid one puts it back; a forged signature is refused; a cancel at period end shows on the plan. A refund AFTER the credit was partly spent is not covered yet (needs a model call in between).
- R1 (35 scripts, no account, no money): 32 pass at the first look; the 3 reds were mine (two mobile paths, one acceptance command) and pass now.
- The server's acceptance plan on the replica: 493 ok and 50 red with the replica's defaults, 523 ok and 25 red once the connections, cloud computers, hosted runs, a welcome credit, the failover seed and the request log were on (`scripts/user-journey-acceptance.sh`). None of the 25 is a product defect found so far: they are the OAuth server and the team-space provider the replica does not have (matrix M1, 503 `*_disabled` or `temporarily_unavailable`), stale expectations (UJ-04, UJ-06, UJ-07, UJ-08, UJ-18) and one contract line (UJ-05).

- J21-08 `scripts/audit-server-mails.mjs` reads the 4 mails the server sends on the flows reachable on a replica (sign-up, sign-in code, password reset, invitation): all 4 have a subject that names the server, one line, no placeholder, the code or the link, the lifetime; the invitation link points at the web origin the server was started with. The mails of the second-factor recovery, a connected social account and the owner's notices are not covered yet.
- J10-02 `scripts/audit-consumption.mjs --scenario models`: 5 mixed calls (tiny to 100k tokens in, 20 to 4,000 out) priced by hand: the answers report the engine's tokens, the usage rows count them exactly, the credit balance goes down by the same amount as the rows, and the multiple (4.000) is identical for input-heavy and output-heavy calls. The one red is UJ-25. Pots, connectors, cloud computer and quote (J08-02, J11-06, J12-07, J12-10) are not built yet.
- J21-04 `scripts/check-links-cross.mjs` (read only, live public pages): the server's legal index lists 6 documents, each answers 200 in English and French; the terms and privacy addresses the app shows are `/legal/<document>` on the server; every legal link on the site's 68 pages goes to that origin; each document names a contact address at the product's domain (or the consumer mediator the terms name on purpose). See UJ-26 for what is missing.
- J20-03 `scripts/test-hostile-strings-live.mjs`: 22 hostile strings (markup, script, template markers, SQL, a path, emoji, Arabic, a combining-mark stack, line break, NUL, tab, bidirectional override, invisible tag text, blanks, 300 and 4,000 letters) into the sign-up display name, the team name at creation and at rename, and the invitation subject: 66 submissions, 39 accepted, 27 refused, 0 server errors, 0 failures. The server cleans control, invisible and override characters instead of refusing them (8 strings are stored changed; see UJ-19); a blank display name falls back to the e-mail's first part. Not covered: skill, automation and chat texts, a shared page (they need a DSH).
- J12-10 `scripts/audit-consumption.mjs --scenario quote`: 60 cloud-computer quotes (3 shapes, 4 volumes, always on and 4 awake-hour counts) fit Fly's public grid x 4, rounded up, under ONE region factor (about 1.034, inferred from the answers, not published); price rises with size, volume and hours, always on = 720 awake hours, `charged` false everywhere, 900 hours refused with 400. Not covered: the other regions, the meter itself.
- J16-03 `scripts/test-automation-schedule-matrix.mjs` is built and passes: 4,715 comparisons of the scheduler's matcher against an independent minute-by-minute oracle (21 expressions, 11 zones, instants on and around each 2026 clock change in four zones, month end, a leap day, Vixie's OR), 0 failures. One decision, not a defect: a job whose wall time the clocks skip (« every hour at :07 » on the spring change) runs at the first instant after the gap (03:00 local), as Vixie cron does.

## Process findings

- 2026-11-04: a sandbox started with the soft-takeoff keeper stops after 1,800 s unless `KB_SANDBOX_TTL=0`; a walker that started late found its sandbox gone.
- 2026-11-04: the server's acceptance plan needs the replica started with `REPLICA_SEED=traffic` (failover, health), `LOG_LEVEL=info` (the log checks) and a welcome credit (Free has no budget). `scripts/user-journey-acceptance.sh` does all three.
- 2026-11-04: `REPLICA_SEED=scenario` cannot run the acceptance plan: it sets `member_limit_without_plan` to 1 and the fixtures need a second member.
- 2026-11-04: the e2e harness (`scripts/e2e-teams-run.sh`) used its DEFAULT ports (8820/8821/3098/3110) on `run`, `preflight` and `replica-reset` even when `up` had been started on others, so the fixed port in the invitation check read 8820 and failed against a harness on 8830; it now reads its ports back from `KB_E2E_API`, `KB_E2E_WEB`, `KB_E2E_HOST_A` and `KB_E2E_HOST_B`.
- 2026-11-04: the harness cases share ONE replica and its state: j5 leaves a usage cap on both accounts (« 100% of your 24 hours allowance is used »), so a case that runs after it fails its preflight. Run the cap cases (J09-08, J10-05, J20-01) each after a `replica-reset` + `soft-takeoff-repair.sh`, or in a plan that orders them first.
- 2026-11-04: the soft-takeoff runner reports « the repository changed during <case> » when the working tree is edited while it runs; edit only between rounds.
