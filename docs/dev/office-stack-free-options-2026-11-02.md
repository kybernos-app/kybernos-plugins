# Build Office-grade editing on ONLYOFFICE's engine

No free stack is both proven and Google/Microsoft-grade across slides, sheets, documents and PDF today, and nobody in this research ran an editor on the owner's real files, so every fidelity statement below is a vendor or author claim until the spikes in the build section pass. On paper exactly one family clears the bar: the **ONLYOFFICE editor engine (AGPL-3.0 plus additional terms)** has a slide editor with an animation pane, custom motion paths, master editing, notes, transitions, charts and comments, a spreadsheet editor with pivot tables, sparklines, conditional formatting and a solver, a document editor with tracked changes and comparison, and a PDF editor with text editing, redaction and forms, all in the free Community edition ([README table](https://github.com/ONLYOFFICE/DocumentServer/blob/HEAD/Readme.md), [changelog](https://github.com/ONLYOFFICE/DocumentServer/blob/HEAD/CHANGELOG.md)). Its problem is delivery and licence, not features: there is no macOS server, the Docker image is **899 MiB compressed while the owner's Mac had 2.9 GiB free**, and its AGPL regime has been publicly disputed since April 2026. The recommended build is an Apache-2.0 file layer the agent drives (OfficeCLI, the LibreOffice kit DSH already carries, optionally ONLYOFFICE Document Builder), the ONLYOFFICE editors shipped as an optional, unmodified, on-demand pack that runs in the browser with no server (the x2t-WASM technique CryptPad already ships) inside a sandboxed iframe, and a separate permissive PDF lane (pdf.js or EmbedPDF). Slides go first, because that is where the contenteditable proof of concept failed: it solved text inside one box, while selection, snapping, masters, animation and presenter mode are most of a slide editor (Inference). Three gates stand between this plan and a default install: written confirmation of the licence reading, a fidelity bake-off on the owner's real files, and a measured footprint on a real Mac. If a gate fails, the fallback is a permissive composite (GenOffice-derived slides, Univer OSS sheets, eigenpal core documents) that is licence-clean but delivers Excel-lite and Word without review, at months of build.

*Labels.* Unlabelled facts were read from a primary source or reproduced in a test. (Snippet) means a search summary or unopened page; (Inference) is my reasoning; (vendor claim) is marketing or a README that nobody tested. This is not legal advice. Effort sizes are my estimates on the earlier report's scale: S is days, M is one to three weeks, L is one to two months; figures in engineer-weeks or months come from the notes and are estimates there too.

| Format | Build-now pick | Permissive fallback |
|---|---|---|
| Slides | ONLYOFFICE presentation editor in the pack | GenOffice Slides, vendored |
| Sheets | ONLYOFFICE spreadsheet editor in the pack | Univer Sheets OSS plus OfficeCLI file layer |
| Docs | ONLYOFFICE document editor in the pack | eigenpal core (Apache-2.0) plus own review layer or the frozen 1.9.0 snapshot |

## Only ONLYOFFICE is deep in all four formats; the permissive picks stop short

### Slides: ONLYOFFICE leads on depth, GenOffice on licence, Collabora on presenting

The owner's checklist (notes, slideshow with presenter view, transitions, animations, masters and themes, slide sorter) is met in changelog or source by four candidates, and observed running in none. ONLYOFFICE 10.0 has had notes since 5.0, custom motion paths since 7.2, an animation pane and master editing since 8.1, 20 added transitions and 25 themes in 9.4, plus notes, outline and reading views and export to video in 10.0 ([changelog](https://github.com/ONLYOFFICE/DocumentServer/blob/HEAD/CHANGELOG.md)). Its presenter mode gives notes, a timer, laser and pen, but the help page documents **no next-slide preview**, and a forum request for one was only forwarded to QA on 2026-05-22 ([help](https://helpcenter.onlyoffice.com/docs/userguides/presentation_editor/PreviewPresentation.aspx), [forum](https://community.onlyoffice.com/t/presentations-editor-presenter-view-show-next-animation/19570)). Collabora's console has the next-slide preview, timer, clock and overview, and 26.04 adds Follow-Me ([source](https://github.com/CollaboraOnline/online.mirror/blob/main/browser/src/slideshow/PresenterConsole.js), [release post](https://www.collaboraonline.com/blog/collabora-office-26-04-release/)), but its PPTX animation fidelity is unproven. GenOffice Slides is the permissive surprise: Apache-2.0, an OOXML-native engine that patches only what the editor touched, and source for transitions including Morph, an animation pane, a master editor and a presenter view with next-slide preview, timer, notes, pen and laser ([README](https://github.com/genspark-ai/genoffice), [PresenterView.tsx](https://github.com/genspark-ai/genoffice/blob/main/apps/slides/src/renderer/components/PresenterView.tsx)). It is also three months old, an Electron app, and its many PPTX bug reports closed within days show fixing speed rather than fidelity ([issues](https://github.com/genspark-ai/genoffice/issues?q=is%3Aissue+pptx)). No candidate documents a PowerPoint-style grid sorter; all give a thumbnail rail with drag-reorder, duplicate and hide. Against Google Slides every option matches the classic desktop model, not Google's collaboration polish or template ecosystem (Inference).

### Sheets: Excel-lite is free; editable charts and pivots are free only inside ONLYOFFICE or LibreOffice engines

Univer's Apache-2.0 Sheets is the best permissive grid: about 500 formula modules, conditional formatting, validation, filter, sort, tables, images and comments, a 1.64 MB gzip preset, and independent use by GenOffice with zero Pro packages ([packages](https://github.com/dream-num/univer/tree/dev/packages), [GenOffice lockfile](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/genspark-ai/genoffice/main/package-lock.json)). Its charts, pivots, sparklines, print, collaboration, history and xlsx import/export are Pro ([registry](https://registry.npmjs.org/@univerjs/preset-sheets-advanced)). The decisive finding is about files, not grids: in a two-sheet fixture, IronCalc's export lost charts, images, comments, tables, validation, autofilter, protection, page setup, tab colour, sparklines and rich-text runs, ExcelJS lost charts, sparklines and dynamic-array metadata, and OfficeCLI 1.0.160 patching in place **lost nothing across 24 parts**, with dependent cached values recalculated (own tests). A UI model saved back through any free converter therefore cannot be faithful, so fidelity must come from patching the original file. In a 62-formula differential test both IronCalc and OfficeCLI's evaluator matched 60, with different failures (same source), so free engines are good on mainstream functions but need a golden suite and a second opinion. ONLYOFFICE's sheet editor lists pivot tables, sparklines, conditional formatting, validation, a solver and sheet views in Community ([README](https://github.com/ONLYOFFICE/DocumentServer/blob/HEAD/Readme.md)); in-grid chart editing was not itemised in the notes and goes into the spike.

### Docs: review features are the dividing line

The question for Word-class is who owns comments, tracked changes, print and PDF export. eigenpal's Apache-2.0 core has real Word-metric pagination, headers and footers, tables, styles, lists and a table of contents, and its own matrix puts 97 of 111 features in the free tier, but **tracked changes, comments, printing, PDF export, collaboration and the Editor API are Pro** under an evaluation licence ([feature matrix](https://github.com/eigenpal/docx-editor/blob/main/docs/site/data/word-features.ts), [Pro licence](https://github.com/eigenpal/docx-editor/blob/main/packages/pro/LICENSE.md)). In the free tier 33 of 97 features cannot be edited, and a Show HN reader called a 40-page paste "quite unusable for writing" (Snippet, [HN](https://hn.svelte.dev/item/48228411)). The June 2026 `@eigenpal/*` 1.9.0 packages are Apache-2.0 and contain tracked-change, comment, collaboration and agent-bridge code, but nobody ran them ([registry](https://registry.npmjs.org/@eigenpal%2fdocx-editor-react)). SuperDoc has the most features and a vanilla-JS API but is AGPL-3.0 ([repo](https://github.com/superdoc-dev/superdoc)) or $6,000 a year for a commercial licence (Snippet, [superdoc.dev](https://www.superdoc.dev/)). ONLYOFFICE's document editor carries comments, tracked changes, comparison, content controls and mail merge in Community ([README](https://github.com/ONLYOFFICE/DocumentServer/blob/HEAD/Readme.md)).

### PDF: annotation, redaction and forms are free; rewriting body text is not

pdf.js (Apache-2.0, monthly releases, `pdfjs-dist` 6.5.438 on 2026-11-01) edits freetext, highlight, stamp, ink and signature annotations ([source](https://github.com/mozilla/pdf.js/tree/master/src/display/editor)). EmbedPDF v2 (MIT, 2.15.1; v3 is Apache-2.0 but still `next`) packages annotation, form, redaction (content actually removed), signature, stamp, search, print and export plugins on a PDFium fork, though its feature quality was not tested ([README](https://github.com/embedpdf/embed-pdf-viewer), [v2 packages](https://github.com/embedpdf/embed-pdf-viewer/tree/v2/packages)). Page operations come from `@cantoo/pdf-lib` (MIT), qpdf and pdfcpu (Apache-2.0), cryptographic signing from `@signpdf/signpdf` (MIT), OCR from tesseract.js (Apache-2.0) or OCRmyPDF (MPL-2.0), and generation from Chromium print with `@page` margin boxes (Chrome 131+, Snippet), Typst (Apache-2.0) or pdfme (MIT) ([Chrome 131](https://developer.chrome.com/release-notes/131), [Typst](https://github.com/typst/typst)). What stays weaker than Acrobat for free: reflowing existing paragraphs with their original fonts, certified signatures with a trust UI, PDF/A and PDF/UA validation, Acrobat-grade form authoring, compare and preflight (Inference). ONLYOFFICE's PDF editor claims text editing, redaction and forms, which would make it the only free native text editor, but that is a README claim that no one tested. Browser editors that "edit text" by covering the original with a background-coloured box are an overlay hack ([vensas](https://github.com/vensas/pdf-editor)), Stirling-PDF's text editor is alpha and the repo mixes MIT with a proprietary user licence ([licence](https://github.com/Stirling-Tools/Stirling-PDF/blob/main/LICENSE)), and MuPDF, Ghostscript (AGPL), poppler and pandoc (GPL) stay out. The mitigation that fits an agent product is to regenerate a PDF from its source and use annotate, redact and fill on received PDFs, rather than edit existing text (Inference). The agent drives all of this with scripts on the file; EmbedPDF's plugin API is the live surface.

## No all-in-one is both free and proven, and ONLYOFFICE is the nearest

ONLYOFFICE is the best single option because it is the only engine that covers all four formats at a depth the changelog documents, keeps OOXML as its native model, processes the document client-side (which is why it can run as a server-less WASM build and why in-page scripts can drive it; Inference), and exposes a free Office API through Document Builder, plugins and macros ([Office API](https://api.onlyoffice.com/docs/office-api/get-started/overview/), [Document Builder](https://github.com/ONLYOFFICE/DocumentBuilder)). Since 9.4.0 (2026-05-19) it also dropped the 20-document limit and collapsed into one process without RabbitMQ or a database ([changelog](https://github.com/ONLYOFFICE/DocumentServer/blob/HEAD/CHANGELOG.md)), and it has 53 listed integrations ([connectors](https://www.onlyoffice.com/all-connectors)). It fails "free and clean" in five ways. The licence is AGPL-3.0 with additional terms and a public FAQ that calls Community "intended for testing, personal use, development, and small-scale deployments" and recommends a commercial licence "for production environments and commercial distribution" ([FAQ](https://helpcenter.onlyoffice.com/docs/faq/docs-community.aspx)). Branding and the About notice must stay, and white-label, the Document Builder HTTP service and the Automation API are Developer-edition only. No macOS server exists, so a Mac means Docker, a VM or a browser-only build. Its sizing pages still describe the old 4 GB setup, so real RAM and startup are unmeasured. And the browser-only builds that avoid Docker are community forks that track ONLYOFFICE or Euro-Office on their own schedule (Inference).

Collabora is the credible second. Its source is MPL-2.0 and 26.04 added an OpenAI-compatible AI assistant for Writer, Calc and Impress, off by default ([coolwsd.xml.in](https://github.com/CollaboraOnline/online.mirror/blob/main/coolwsd.xml.in), [26.04](https://linuxiac.com/collabora-online-26-04-office-suite-released-with-optional-ai-assistant/)), but the free CODE build is labelled not recommended for production, carries a non-open-source brand package and an unremovable welcome layer (or 20 connections and 10 documents in `home_mode`), has no macOS server, and every non-Collabora source rates its PowerPoint and Word fidelity lower, with reviews and vendor claims rather than benchmarks as the only evidence ([CODE page](https://www.collaboraonline.com/code/), [source](https://github.com/CollaboraOnline/online.mirror/blob/main/wsd/COOLWSD.cpp), [docker README](https://github.com/CollaboraOnline/online.mirror/blob/main/docker/README)). GenOffice is the only permissive all-in-one codebase (Docs, Sheets, Slides, PDF, CLI and MCP on one engine layer, Univer OSS only for the Sheets grid), but it is an Electron suite, not a component, with private packages and no run evidence. Univer is the best single SDK and the best agent API, but its charts, pivots, import/export and Slides are Pro, and Pro is evaluation-only for production. Euro-Office, CryptPad and Nextcloud Office are ruled out as bases: Euro-Office trails upstream at 9.3.x and sits in the licence dispute, CryptPad is an end-to-end-encrypted product with no plugins or macros, and Nextcloud needs a Linux PHP and database stack.

## Build now: an Apache file layer, an isolated ONLYOFFICE pack and a PDF lane

### The file is the model; editors are isolated panels

Every credible system in the notes keeps one structured source of truth, applies small addressed atomic operations, validates, renders and looks, and has an explicit way to reconcile human edits; Google's own Slides API shows the pattern with atomic `batchUpdate` and `requiredRevisionId` ([batchUpdate](https://developers.google.com/workspace/slides/api/reference/rest/v1/presentations/batchUpdate)). Applied here, the real OOXML or PDF file on disk is the source of truth, because every free UI engine models only a subset of OOXML (Inference from the round-trip tests above).

| Layer | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Agent edits | OfficeCLI (Apache-2.0, pinned, on-demand, behind a thin wrapper) for path-addressed atomic edits and validate; ONLYOFFICE Document Builder (AGPL, pip, optional) where the Office API is needed and the editor's own engine should compute layout; docx.js and PptxGenJS (MIT) for creating files from scratch | xlsx patching verified lossless in my test; OfficeCLI's pptx and docx claims (typed masters, morph and p15 transitions, 31 animation presets) are vendor claims, so S2 tests them |
| Person edits | The ONLYOFFICE pack in a sandboxed iframe on its own origin; postMessage in (`document:open-url`), out (`document:opened`, `document:saved` with file bytes); save route under the existing write guard | The Manus pattern of one isolated engine per format fences React skew and global CSS; ranuts/document documents this embed protocol ([reference](https://github.com/ranuts/document/blob/main/docs/reference.md)) |
| Agent and person together | A snapshot per agent write, a content-hash check before reload, a conflict rather than a clobber when the person has unsaved edits, a diff or approve step | Draft-and-approve is how `dsh-univer-office` and GenOffice avoid overwrites (Inference for the hash rule) |
| Previews | The editor is the preview for open documents; Creations and Files use the engine's read-only `documentPreviews` seam; the agent's render-and-look step uses OfficeCLI or the LibreOffice kit | Zero engine patches (AGENTS.md rule 1) |
| Exports | PDF from the pack's own engine so page breaks match what the person saw; native PPTX/DOCX/XLSX files; ONLYOFFICE 10.0 also exports video or animated image | LibreOffice and ONLYOFFICE are two layout engines, so a LibreOffice PDF may paginate differently (Inference) |
| Packaging | Pack and fonts pulled on demand with pinned hashes, never in git; every client factory wrapped in try/catch | AGENTS.md rules 2 and 4; the core client already sits at 3,739,209 of 4,194,304 bytes ([earlier audit](../reports/Office%20editing%20stack%20for%20agents.md)) |
| PDF | EmbedPDF v2 or pdf.js panel; pdf-lib fork, qpdf, pdfcpu, tesseract on the file | Permissive, independent of the licence gate |
| Escape hatch | "Open in desktop editor": ONLYOFFICE Desktop Editors (macOS arm64 dmg 324 MB, `--edit "path"` CLI, no remote-control API) or free Collabora Office (Mac App Store, 283 MB) | Cheap, but whether either reloads a file changed on disk is unknown ([ONLYOFFICE CLI](https://api.onlyoffice.com/docs/desktop-editors/usage-api/command-line-flags/)) |

The browser-only pack deserves one explanation. CryptPad 2026.10.0 runs the ONLYOFFICE sheet, document and presentation editors in the browser and converts files with `onlyoffice-x2t-wasm`, with no server beyond static hosting ([CryptPad](https://github.com/cryptpad/cryptpad), [x2t-wasm](https://github.com/cryptpad/onlyoffice-x2t-wasm)); ranuts/document (AGPL-3.0, 2,013 stars, v0.0.9 on 2026-11-02) packages the same idea as an embeddable static site with a postMessage API, a WebMCP tool set and an experimental AI assistant that creates slides ([README](https://github.com/ranuts/document), [releases](https://github.com/ranuts/document/releases)). Two things must be learned from the pack's own About dialog in spike S1: which engine version it carries, because CryptPad from version 9 onward is based on Euro-Office (9.3.x, without the 9.4 transitions and themes or the 10.0 notes view) ([CryptPad OO README](https://github.com/cryptpad/cryptpad/blob/main/www/common/onlyoffice/README.md)), and whether the Office API or plugins are reachable by an injected script, which no source confirms. The animation pane and master editing date from 8.1, so a 9.x build should still have the core slide features (Inference).

### Legal checks before anything ships by default

Five checks are explicitly required, and three more follow from the notes. The AGPL reading rests on the FSF FAQ, which says sockets, pipes and command-line arguments are normally communication between separate programs, and that exchanging complex internal data structures can merge them ([GPL FAQ](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-faq.html)); AGPL section 13 as shipped applies "if you modify the Program" ([LICENSE](https://github.com/ONLYOFFICE/DocumentServer/blob/HEAD/LICENSE)).

| Item | Finding | Check | Safe rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| AGPL engine as a separate unmodified process | Iframe URL, postMessage of file bytes and files on disk are the "separate programs" case; copying code into the repo is not (Inference) | Counsel confirms the iframe pack reading and who conveys the binary | Unmodified; Kybernos pulls the artifact on the user's request (Inference: avoids Kybernos conveying it); never copy sdkjs or web-apps into the Apache repo or JS bundle; any patch makes it a modified version |
| ONLYOFFICE additional terms | Retain notices; a clearly accessible UI feature must identify ONLYOFFICE as original developer; modified versions flagged; no trademark licence; non-code assets CC BY-SA 4.0; breach may end rights. The public FAQ still says the logo may not be removed, while the FSF (Snippet, April 2026) calls logo retention a removable restriction and ONLYOFFICE disputes that; no court has ruled ([LICENSE](https://github.com/ONLYOFFICE/DocumentServer/blob/HEAD/LICENSE), [licence FAQ](https://www.onlyoffice.com/license-faq), [Linuxiac](https://linuxiac.com/fsf-says-onlyoffice-cannot-use-agpl-to-restrict-forks/), [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2026/04/02/eurooffice_forks_onlyoffice/)) | Get written answers from Ascensio on four points: unmodified Community launched by an Apache-2.0 desktop app on the user's machine; the static pack; what "commercial distribution" requires; naming and trademark. ## Iterate later: close the Google gaps once the loop works

The first iteration targets the gaps the research flagged. A live bridge into the open editor replaces file reload if S3 shows the Office API reachable, using free plugins loaded through `pluginsData` as a separate work (Inference; ONLYOFFICE's own plugin repo is AGPL, so copy nothing) ([plugins config](https://api.onlyoffice.com/docs/docs-api/usage-api/config/editor/plugins/)). A person-to-agent comment loop (select a cell, paragraph or slide region, comment, and the comment reaches the agent) is cheap and high-value, needs only the file's own comments, and matches the region-annotation loop OpenAI's desktop app already offers ([OpenAI Learn](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/artifacts-viewer?surface=web)). If ONLYOFFICE still lacks a next-slide preview, write a Kybernos presenter console over rendered slides, following the Apache-2.0 pattern in GenOffice's presenter view. A thumbnail-grid slide sorter and a theme and template library cover Google's ecosystem gap; 9.4 already shipped 25 themes. Version history needs per-agent-turn snapshots, because the browser-only editors "keep no local history". Collabora can enter behind an adapter as a second engine for PPTX cross-checks, and its OpenAI-compatible assistant could point at the DSH gateway, though slide-generation quality is unverified (Inference) ([Linuxiac](https://linuxiac.com/collabora-online-26-04-office-suite-released-with-optional-ai-assistant/)). Windows and Linux users could get the full Docs sidecar. For the Acrobat ceiling (certified signatures, PDF/A and PDF/UA, form authoring, compare), the open-core answer is a private paid module. Paid engines (Univer Pro, eigenpal Pro, ONLYOFFICE Developer) stay optional modules behind the same adapter, and only if the owner pays and obtains written redistribution terms.

## Conclusion

The pattern across all four formats is that every permissive editor stops at the same line: charts, pivots, review, print, import/export and collaboration are exactly the features each open-core vendor (Univer, eigenpal, Tiptap) moves behind a paywall or an evaluation licence, so an all-Apache stack is Excel-lite and Word-without-review by construction. ONLYOFFICE is the exception because it is copyleft rather than open core: it charges in obligations, not fees. That turns a technical choice into a legal and packaging decision, which is why the three gates matter more than any feature comparison and why the permissive fallback must stay alive rather than be abandoned.

The file-first principle (the agent edits the real file, then renders and looks) survives and gets sharper: fidelity is won at the file, not in the UI model, since every free grid or document model covers only a subset of OOXML. What changes is the human layer. A full Office-grade editor is reachable for free in the ONLYOFFICE pack, and the first day of spikes (S1, then S2 on the owner's three real decks) will tell, with real files rather than vendor pages, whether to build on it or take the slower Apache-clean road.
