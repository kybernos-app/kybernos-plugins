# Haiku writing guide

A haiku is a short poem of three lines. The first line has five syllables, the second has seven and the third has five again.

## How to write one

1. Pick a season word (snow, cherry blossom, cicada) so the poem is anchored in a moment.
2. Describe one small image, with no explanation. Let the reader feel the rest.
3. Count the syllables aloud. Adjust a word until each line fits its count.
4. Cut the last line if it only repeats the first two.

## Style

- Prefer concrete nouns to abstractions.
- Avoid rhyme: haiku rarely rhyme.
- A pause (a dash or a line break) between the second and third line gives the poem its turn.
- Read the finished poem once more and remove one word.

Offer three versions when the person gives you a theme, and let them choose the one they like best.
