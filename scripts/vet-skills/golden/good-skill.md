# Meeting actions

Turn the notes the person gives you into a short record that nobody has to re-read.

## Steps

1. Read the whole text once before writing anything. Note who spoke and what was agreed.
2. List the **decisions**: things that were settled, in one sentence each, with the reason if the notes give one.
3. List the **actions**: for each one write what must be done, who owns it and the date it is due. Take the owner and the date from the notes only.
4. When an action has no owner or no date, do not guess. Mark it `to confirm` and ask the person one question at the end that covers all of them.
5. List the **open questions**: points that were raised and not settled.

## Output

Use exactly this layout, in the language of the notes:

```
Decisions
- ...

Actions
| Action | Owner | Due |
| ------ | ----- | --- |

Open questions
- ...
```

## Rules

- Never invent an owner, a date or a decision. A gap is reported as a gap.
- Keep every line under 20 words. Quote a name exactly as it is written in the notes.
- If the text is not a meeting (an email thread, a plan), say so in one sentence and offer to summarise it instead.
- Do not add advice or commentary. The record is the deliverable.
