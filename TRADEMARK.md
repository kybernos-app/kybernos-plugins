**Kybernos** is a trademark owned by the Kybernos project / its operators.
This policy governs the use of the "Kybernos" name and related marks, and is
**separate and independent** from the Apache-2.0 license that governs the code.

## Purpose

The code in this repository is open source (Apache-2.0) and may be used,
modified, and redistributed freely. This trademark policy is the mechanism by
which the Kybernos brand is protected — it does not restrict the code license,
and it does not rely on non-OSI license clauses (no Commons Clause, no SSPL/BSL).

## Rules

1. **You may** use the "Kybernos" name to refer to the open-source project,
   to contribute to it, to build compatible tools, and to state that your
   software integrates with or is based on Kybernos.

2. **You may NOT** offer a hosted, managed, or resold service under the
   "Kybernos" brand, such as "Kybernos Cloud" or "Kybernos as a Service",
   without explicit written permission from the trademark owner.

3. **You may NOT** use the Kybernos name, logo, or marks in a way that
   suggests endorsement, sponsorship, or affiliation with the Kybernos
   project when that is not the case, or in a way that could confuse users
   about the origin of a product or service.

4. **You may NOT** use the Kybernos name in domain names, product names, or
   service names that compete with the Kybernos project's own offerings, or
   that misrepresent the relationship between your product and Kybernos.

## Team branding

The product can show an organisation's own name and logo (a "Team brand") in
place of the Kybernos name and logo, for example in the sidebar and in the
browser tab title. This is permitted, and does not need written permission,
when all of the following hold:

1. The mention **Powered by Kybernos**, with the Kybernos wordmark, stays
   visible next to the Team brand in the sidebar. The Team brand cannot remove,
   hide or alter it: the application adds it whenever a Team brand is active.
2. The browser tab icon stays the Kybernos "K". A Team brand does not replace it.
3. The Team brand does not claim, or suggest, that the organisation is the
   Kybernos project or is endorsed by it (rule 3 above still applies).

Offering a hosted or managed service under the Kybernos name is still covered
by rule 2 above, with or without a Team brand.

## Apps built from this source

The phone app in `kybernos-host/` is open source like the rest. You may build it for yourself, study it, and publish a modified version, but an app you publish (on an app store or anywhere else) must **not**
use the name "Kybernos", the Kybernos "K" logo (the red K with three dots, drawn by `kbBrandArt` in `packages/kybernos-plugin/client.js` and by `kybernos-host/app/scripts/brand-art.mjs`) or the Kybernos
bundle identifiers (`app.kybernos.host`): give it your own name, icon and identifiers, and say that it is based on Kybernos. The official apps are the ones published by the Kybernos
maintainers; they are signed with keys that are not in this repository. Rules 2 to 4 above apply to apps like any other product.

## Enforcement

This policy is enforced at the sole discretion of the trademark owner. If you
have questions about whether a proposed use is permitted, contact the owner
before using the mark. Formal trademark registration may be pursued at a later
date when the project has a real user base; this policy applies now regardless
of registration status.
